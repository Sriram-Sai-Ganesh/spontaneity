## Team Values

- **Accountability and Reliability:** We commit to upholding our collaboration values and holding one another accountable. Members are expected to attend all meetings on time, fully prepared, and to complete assigned tasks reliably to avoid blocking the team’s progress. We understand there may be times when members cannot attend scheduled meetings and agree to communicate at least 2 hours before meetings without deliverables or two days before meetings where members need to present their progress.
- **Teamwork & Quality:** We share collective responsibility for the quality of our deliverables. We prioritize team success over individual correctness and remain open to compromise.
- **Communication & Respect:** We will listen actively, provide non-judgmental feedback, and respect personal and cultural differences. Every member is encouraged to voice their ideas and concerns openly. Members are encouraged to express their opinions and discontent with decisions and we will work on a majority-vote basis to determine project & meeting decisions.
- **Conflict Resolution:** We address differences immediately and honestly. Conflicts should be resolved directly between the individuals involved before escalating to a manager or instructor. In the event that members are not reachable or the conflict is not resolved, the group agrees to try contacting the member in question thrice before escalating the situation to the TAs and instructor as needed.

## Team Communication

- Developers will meet every 3 days to review their progress and plans. Additionally, they will have a weekly meeting with clients/managers (or advisor/instructor) to discuss the project status. As part of the team-only meetings, the aim of these meetings is to compose our concerns and document our progress to present to the TA/instructors for the weekly meeting.
- Everyone is expected to actively participate in team meetings. If unable to attend a meeting, advance notice should be given as described in the team values' accountability & reliability section. If a team member misses a meeting, they should follow up within 12 hours to check if there are any pending tasks.
- At the beginning of each iteration, an availability schedule will be provided. Team members will be available for deployment/delivery outside regular business hours or on weekends.
- Slack will be the primary communication tool, while texting via cell phone will be used for urgent correspondence.
- A response on Slack is expected within 12 hours. If a response is not received in time, the person responsible will buy coffee for everyone!
  - Use the 👍 emoji to indicate agreement or acknowledgment.
- Engage in a conversation in a dedicated thread (click “Reply to thread”) for questions, comments, objections, etc.
  - Use the ✅ emoji to show that action has been taken in response to a thread.
  - Use the 👀 emoji to indicate that you will look into the matter later.
- Respectful communication: Treat teammates, clients, and course staff with respect. Encourage diverse viewpoints and give everyone space to contribute. Disagreement is normal; keep it constructive and focused on the work.
- Provide constructive critiques that improve the project and team process. Be specific about what can be changed and why, and suggest alternatives. Team members agree to be respectful in verbalizing their concerns while also providing a suitable alternative that explains how their concern is addressed. The feedback culture aims to give regular, respectful feedback on teamwork, technical quality, and process. Take time to reflect on feedback and act on it.
- Roles and responsibilities: Make sure everyone understands their responsibilities for each iteration. Divide tasks transparently, document ownership, and offer support when someone is stuck or overloaded.
- Use the tools and workflows agreed upon by the team (disclose any AI-assisted development practices). Propose changes openly, discuss trade-offs, and adopt changes only after team agreement.
- Personal responsibility: Own your tasks, deliver on commitments, and communicate early when you need help. Try reasonable solutions before escalating, and bring options to the team when raising a problem.
- Do not share confidential project information outside the team without permission. Follow course rules and JHU academic integrity policies, including any requirements for documenting AI usage, citing sources, and ensuring submitted work reflects the team’s understanding.
